📡 Telco Customer Churn MLOps Pipeline

Production-grade MLOps workflow fortified with automated data validation, explainable AI (XAI), and fairness evaluation gates.

🎯 The "Why"

A high-accuracy churn prediction model becomes a production liability if it silently degrades on drifted data, operates as an unexplainable black box, or discriminates against protected customer cohorts. Drawing on 10 years of Software Quality Assurance and Engineering Productivity experience, this project embeds rigorous evaluation, explainability, and fairness checks as mandatory quality gates across an end-to-end MLOps pipeline.

🛠️ End-to-End Architecture & Evaluation Gates

  • Pre-Ingestion Data Quality Gate (Great Expectations & SQLAlchemy): Enforced automated schema contracts, null-threshold rules, and statistical distribution checks prior to pipeline ingestion.
  • Experiment Tracking & Threshold Tuning (MLflow & Scikit-Learn / XGBoost): Logged reproducible hyperparameter runs, model artifacts, and customized decision thresholds aligned with business retention costs on imbalanced telco data.
  • Explainable AI & Fairness Auditing (SHAP & Fairlearn): Evaluated global and local feature attributions via SHAP while auditing demographic parity and equalized odds across customer segments using Fairlearn.
  • Production Serving & Drift Monitoring (FastAPI, Docker, Evidently AI): Containerized real-time inference endpoints with automated data and concept drift evaluation reports.

💡 Key Engineering & Evaluation Insights

1. Data Quality as the First Line of Model Evaluation

Most silent production ML failures originate from upstream schema shifts rather than model weights. Catching anomalous feature distributions at ingestion with Great Expectations prevents garbage-in inference downstream.

2. Accuracy Without Explainability Blocks Business Action

Knowing a customer has a 78% churn probability is not enough for retention teams. Pairing predictions with SHAP values reveals the exact drivers (such as month-to-month contract spikes or tech-support friction) so business interventions are targeted.

3. Continuous Evaluation Beyond Deployment

Deployment is the starting line of model reliability. Integrating Fairlearn pre-release audits with Evidently AI post-deployment drift monitoring ensures the system remains both fair and statistically valid over time.
